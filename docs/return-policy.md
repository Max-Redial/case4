# Returns policy

Upload this document to the knowledge base as `.txt` (the app does not accept `.md`). The assistant must answer only from it.

## Return window

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Day 30 counts. The window starts on the day the order is delivered, not the day it ships.

## What can be returned

- Apparel and footwear, unworn, with tags attached.
- Items in their original packaging.

## What cannot be returned

- Final sale items.
- Items that have been worn, washed or damaged by the customer.

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
- Original shipping costs are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or was not what the customer ordered.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color are free within the same 30-day window, subject to stock.

## Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged or wrong, the customer should contact us within 7 days of delivery. A person handles these.

## Orders not yet delivered

An order that has not been delivered cannot be returned. The customer can refuse delivery or wait for it to arrive and then start a return.

## Not covered

This policy says nothing about gift cards, international orders, price adjustments or warranty claims.
